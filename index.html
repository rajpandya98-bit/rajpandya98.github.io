About

I work at the intersection of quality engineering and process automation. My background is in GMP-regulated manufacturing and validation — root cause analysis, risk assessment, documentation control — and I use that same structured thinking to design tools that reduce manual review time and human error in regulated workflows. The projects below range from a rule-based decision-support engine built with AI-assisted design, to closed-loop workflow automation, to a clinical-style validation build in MATLAB.
